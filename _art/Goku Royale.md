The theme of the tournament was the "Goku Royale" where each entrant was a 'goku', which in this case was any sort of mishmash of existing characters remixed to avoid copyright or something from the public domain. My Entrant was a combination of Spongebob, Homer Simpson, and Harry Potter, and motifs from [House](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Leaves>) of Leaves. It was very silly and fairly rushed and while I don't think it's my best work I am preserving it here because I was proud of it at one point and that's worth something.

Content warning: Strong language
