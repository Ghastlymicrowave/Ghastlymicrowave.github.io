### Toha Heavy Industries
![textured render](/assets/art/2020Liminal/tohaHeavyIndustries.png)
![untextured render](/assets/art/2020Liminal/toha_heavy_industries.png)
I made this render based off of this panel from the manga BLAME
![](/assets/art/2020Liminal/ref.jpg)
I've adored the larger-than-life architecture in the manga of Tsutomu Nihei ever since I found his work. I felt inspired to make This render in homage.

### Submachine-esque rooms
![](/assets/art/2020Liminal/rooms.jpg)
Another point of inspiration was the '[Submachine](https://www.mateuszskutnik.com/submachine/)' game series by [Mateusz Skutnik](https://www.mateuszskutnik.com/).
Some of these rooms that I drew I also decided to recreate in blender. I would like to revisit this idea in the future.
